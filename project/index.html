Final Project: The Best Recipes

Developer: Sophia and Victoria
Course: Web Design and Development using AI Tools

Project Overview

This website is a fun digital cookbook filled with recipes and helpful meal-planning tools. Each recipe includes a description, ingredient list, and easy-to-follow instructions so users can confidently prepare the meal. Users can also create a checklist of ingredients they need for shopping or cooking. The site makes finding and preparing new meals simple and enjoyable.
