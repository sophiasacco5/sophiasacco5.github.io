The Story Behind the Recipe

Spaghetti and meatballs has always been a special meal in my family because of our Sunday night dinners. My dad used to make spaghetti and meatballs for my family and me, and it quickly became something I looked forward to every week.

More than just a dinner, this meal represents time spent together at the table after a busy week. The smell of the sauce cooking and everyone gathering for dinner made Sunday nights feel comforting and memorable. This recipe is inspired by those family dinners and the tradition that made them so special.
