Mac and Cheese

Recipe #1

The Story Behind the Recipe

Mac and cheese has always been one of my favorite comfort foods. When I was little, I would come home from school after getting off the bus and look forward to the warm bowl of mac and cheese my mom would make for me.

This recipe is a twist on the mac and cheese I grew up eating. It keeps the creamy, cheesy comfort of my childhood favorite while adding a few extra touches to make it feel a little more special. Every time I make it, it reminds me of coming home after a long school day and having a simple meal that always made me feel better.
